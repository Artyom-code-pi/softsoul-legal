Delete your SoftSoul account

You can delete your SoftSoul account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open SoftSoul and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is suspended straight away. You have 7 days to change your mind: sign back in during that time and you can cancel the deletion.

Can't open the app?

Email softsoul.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm and start the deletion. We reply within one month, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

After the 7-day waiting period, your account is permanently removed: your profile, chats, memories, calendar events, weekly summaries and call transcripts, and your messages to us. Limited copies may stay in backups or service providers' logs until their normal retention periods end. Subscription records are kept only while the account exists.

If you asked to be emailed when SoftSoul launches, we keep that email address for that one message until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
