Is SoftSoul free?

Yes, it's free to start. There's an optional paid plan, Plus, for people who want more.

Is Soul a therapist?

No. Soul is an AI friend, not therapy and not medical advice. If you're struggling, she'll encourage you to reach out to real people and will point you to crisis lines.

What does Soul remember, and who can see it?

She remembers the things that matter in your chats, like names, plans and worries. You can see every memory in the app and delete any of it, or delete your whole account. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I talk to her out loud?

Yes. You can text, or start a voice call and just talk.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone, from the App Store. Android is coming soon. We're launching in the UK first.

Who is it for?

Adults, 18 and over.
