What Soul does

Three simple things, done really well.

💬

Text or call

Type when you're on the bus. Call when you'd rather talk. Soul answers in a warm voice and you can talk over her, like with a person.

🧡

She remembers

Names, plans, the thing you were worried about on Sunday. You can see every memory, and tell her to forget anything.

🌤️

She checks in

A good-morning, a "how did it go?", a quiet evening note. You choose how often, or switch it off.

Built with care

A friend on your side, not a yes-machine.

Honest with you

Soul is kind, but she won't just agree with everything. If you ask what she really thinks, she tells you.

Your memories, your call

See what Soul remembers about you, delete any of it, or delete your whole account at any time. Your messages are stored encrypted.

Help when it's serious

If you're going through something heavy, Soul points you to real people and to crisis lines for your country.

Made for adults

SoftSoul is for people aged 18 and over. Soul isn't a therapist, and she never pretends to be one.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number. SoftSoul is company in between. It is not therapy and not a replacement for the people in your life.

Questions people ask

Is SoftSoul free?

Yes, it's free to start, and the beta is free. There's an optional paid plan, Plus, for people who want more.

Is Soul a therapist?

No. Soul is an AI friend, not therapy and not medical advice. If you're struggling, she'll encourage you to reach out to real people and will point you to crisis lines.

What does Soul remember, and who can see it?

She remembers the things that matter in your chats, like names, plans and worries. You can see every memory in the app and delete any of it, or delete your whole account. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I talk to her out loud?

Yes. You can text, or start a voice call and just talk.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android. The beta is open now, and the App Store version is on its way.

Who is it for?

Adults, 18 and over.
