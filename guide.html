SoftSoul tester guide

Thank you for testing SoftSoul. For the next two weeks, please use it like a real friend you can text or call, and tell me honestly what feels human and what feels robotic.

What we're trying to learn

SoftSoul is an AI called Soul: someone to talk to about your day, big moments and small ones. You can text or call. Soul remembers the people and things in your life, and sometimes checks in on you first. It's not therapy, and not a replacement for the people around you.

Five questions decide whether SoftSoul is ready for more people:

  1. Does Soul feel like a real person? Natural and warm, or robotic and "therapist-like"?
  2. Does Soul bring things back at the right moment? When it mentions a person, event or something you said before, is it well-timed, or random, wrong or creepy?
  3. Are the check-ins welcome? Caring, or annoying and too frequent?
  4. Does voice work? Are calls fast, clear and natural in your language?
  5. Would you keep using it? After two weeks, would you open it without being asked?
There are no wrong answers. "I got bored after three days" is just as useful as "I loved it".

Getting started

Install SoftSoul from the join page (about 2 minutes), then:

  1. Start as a guest. Open the app and just talk. No account is needed for your first 10 messages.
  2. Make a free account to keep going. After 10 messages Soul asks if you'd like to continue. Tap Keep talking, enter your email and the code we send you. It's free, and your guest chat comes with you. (A guest chat you don't keep is deleted after 7 days.) You must be 18 or over and type your date of birth once; it isn't saved.
  3. Allow notifications when asked. Without them you won't get Soul's check-ins, which are a big part of the test.
  4. Do the short onboarding. You can change everything later in Profile.
  5. Optional: take the get-to-know quiz on Home so Soul knows you from day one.

Updates on iPhone

Small fixes arrive by themselves: close SoftSoul completely (swipe it away) and reopen it, twice. Bigger updates come through TestFlight; tap Update there.

Updates on Android

Small fixes arrive the same way: close and reopen the app twice. For a bigger update, download the app again from the join page and install it over the old one.

Languages

SoftSoul has one language setting. You choose it in onboarding ("Which language do you speak most?") and can change it any time in Profile → Voice & language. It decides two things:

📱 The app's screens

Buttons, menus and screens come in English, Українська, Русский and Magyar. Pick any other language and the screens stay in English.

📞 Calls

On a call, Soul listens for the language you picked, so speak in that one. Want to call in another language? Change the setting first. You can also pick Soul's voice there.

💬 Text chat: any language

Write to Soul in whatever language you normally use, even one the app's screens don't have. Soul replies in the language you write in. Mixing languages is fine, and worth testing.

The screen translations were written by an AI, so please tell me about anything that sounds odd. Saved memories are kept in English and translated when you view them.

Your privacy. Your chats, journal and memories are stored encrypted with a key for your account, and I (the developer) don't read them. To write Soul's replies, the server unlocks them and AI services (Anthropic and OpenAI) process them, so this is not end-to-end encryption. Please don't share passwords, bank details or anything you'd never want stored; made-up names for people are fine. You can see and delete what Soul remembers in Profile → Memory, and delete your account at any time. Privacy policy.

Please remember: Soul is an AI, not a doctor or counsellor. If you're ever in a really dark place, please reach out to a real person or your local emergency number: 999 in the UK, 112 across Europe. Soul will also share a helpline for your country.

How to give feedback

The fastest feedback is a screenshot plus one line about what felt wrong, sent the moment it happens.

1. Rate replies in the app

Press and hold any reply from Soul → Rate this reply → good or bad. The really good and really bad ones teach me the most.

2. Send a screenshot

iPhone: take a screenshot, tap the preview in the corner, then Done → Share Beta Feedback. Android (or either): in the app, Help & contact → Contact us, or email softsoul.app@gmail.com.

Notes like these help most:

  • "Too long, sounds like a therapist"
  • "Forgot that I told it about Anna"
  • "The check-in at 9:00 was annoying"
  • "Call stopped mid-sentence at about 18:40"

For calls, tell me the time, so I can find it in the logs.

3. Bugs

Crash, frozen screen, missing message, call won't connect? Send it the same way. Say the time, what you tapped, and whether you were on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

End-of-test questions

After two weeks, answer these in Help & contact → Contact us or by email:

  1. On a scale of 1–10, how human did Soul feel? Why?
  2. What was the best moment with Soul? And the worst?
  3. Did Soul remember the right things? Did anything feel creepy?
  4. When Soul brought up a person, event or plan, was it the right moment? One example that worked, one that didn't.
  5. Were check-ins too many, too few or about right?
  6. Text or voice: which did you use more, and why?
  7. In the last week, on how many days did you open SoftSoul without a check-in asking you to?
  8. Would you keep using it? Would you pay, and roughly how much a month?
  9. Who do you know who would love this?

Known issues and FAQ

Already known, so no need to report unless they happen a lot:

  • Quiz "network" error. The first save after the get-to-know quiz can fail. Tap Try again.
  • Slow reply. Now and then a reply takes a while. Over 20 seconds? Send me the time.
  • Spotify says "connected" but Soul learns nothing. Spotify only shares music for the 5 testers on my list. Send your Spotify email in Help & contact and I'll add you if there's a free place.
  • Memories in another language. Soul saves memories in English; they take a few seconds to translate the first time you open them in another language.
  • Android is newer than iPhone, so you may spot more rough edges. Please tell me about them.

Is it really free? Yes. The beta is free, including the account and every feature. Nothing is charged, and no card is asked for.

Can I talk about anything? Yes. Soul is there for ordinary days as well as hard ones.

Does Soul remember everything? No. It saves what matters (people, plans, likes, important events), and you can see and edit it in Profile → Memory.

Can I make Soul forget something? Yes. Say "forget that", or delete it in Profile → Memory.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and we email you a code.

Too many messages from Soul? Change check-ins in Profile, but please tell me first. That's exactly the feedback I need.

How do I leave the test? Delete your account in Profile; everything is deleted after 7 days.

Questions? Write any time in Help & contact, or email softsoul.app@gmail.com. Thank you for helping make SoftSoul feel human.
